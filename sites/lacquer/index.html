Good questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Most days, yes — usually before noon. Booking is safer on weekends, and we release cancellations to the waitlist first.

What's a structured manicure?

A thin layer of builder gel is used to reinforce the natural nail and correct the apex. It looks like a normal manicure and wears for three to four weeks.

Can I bring my own polish?

Of course. There's no fee for it — we just can't guarantee wear time on formulas we haven't used before.

What's the cancellation policy?

Free up to 24 hours before. Inside that window we charge 50%, because the chair usually stays empty.
