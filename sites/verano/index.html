The roast

We roast light enough
to taste the farm.

Every lot lands green and gets cupped three times before it earns a profile. We roast in 12kg batches on a 1974 Probat, log every curve, and rest the beans four days before they leave the building.

  • Cupped and scored in-house, every single lot
  • Prices paid 40% above the commodity market
  • Bagged the day it ships — never warehoused
