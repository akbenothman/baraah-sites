The room

One fire.
No gas, no shortcuts.

We cook everything — the bread, the fish, the vegetables, occasionally the dessert — over oak and vine cuttings. The hearth sits in the middle of the room, which means the whole place smells like dinner and you can watch your food happen.

The menu is written each morning after the market. If something ran out, it ran out. We think that's the point.

Amara Vela Chef & owner
